`/orchestrator:questions` gathers every autopilot flow's pending questions in the repo, puts each issue's questions to you with a second recommendation beside the session's, posts your reply and lifts `needs-human` (#1571). Afterwards it offers to retry stopped phases and to lift the label on issues already replied to. New verbs back it: `orch.sh autopilot questions` lists each `needs-human` issue as a question, a reply or a stop; `autopilot reply <issue> <file>` posts the reply and removes `needs-human`; `autopilot resume <issue>` removes the label alone. `orch.sh pr read <issue> <file>` reads the body and diff of the open PR that closes or refers to the issue. `GLOSSARY.md` gains the **Pending question** entry.
