bump: patch

The ticket commands now read one ticket's facts once through `ticket_facts <n> <prefix>`, which sets the ticket worktree's path and state, its checked-out branch, the checkout's branch, and the ticket's own branch and whether it exists in variables the caller names, and writes no other variable; the refusals live in require helpers that read those facts. The teardown takes the facts its caller read and reports which removal step failed, so `ticket reclaim` words its per-ticket lines without probing again. `ticket close <n>` (without `--unmerged`), `ticket merge <n>` and `ticket-worktree add <n>` now die before acting where git's record of the checkouts cannot be read; nothing else a user of `orch.sh` can observe changes.
