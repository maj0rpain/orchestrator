In an autopilot session `phase advance` validates the posted handoff, moves the phase label, then releases the claim (deleting `orch:running`, then the claim ref last); `review ready` moves the label to `orch:done` and releases the claim; `spec pause --round` releases the claim. `autopilot ask <phase> <file>` posts a question comment that @mentions the handle, adds `needs-human` and releases the claim, and `autopilot answer <phase> <file>` writes the question and the replies after it. `doctor` flags a phase label that disagrees with the recorded phase and lists other hosts' claims older than a day, and `doctor --flow` refuses an `ORCH_AUTOPILOT=1` flow whose issue has no phase label.
