In an autopilot flow a handoff is one issue comment tagged `<!-- orch:handoff <name> -->`: `handoff post` and `handoff fetch` write and read it, `handoff path` fetches it into a scratch file, and `01-plan.md` gains an `## Autopilot` block (`Rounds`, `Budget`) that `handoff validate` checks only in autopilot. `autopilot rounds` and `autopilot budget` read it, and `autopilot start <issue> <plan-file>` posts the plan comment and adds `orch:spec`. The `orch-handoff` skill posts with `handoff post` in autopilot.
